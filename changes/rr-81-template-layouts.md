RR-81 (OPF 0.19 layout templates; output-changing for slides on a template only): every 0.19 template a deck's slides use (`areas`/`regions`, embedded under `catalogs.<group>.layouts` or from a registered catalog) becomes one native `p:sldLayout` under the slide's master, named by the record's `name` and typed as PowerPoint knows it (`cover` is `title`, `section` is `secHead`, `text`, `list`, `agenda` and `faq` are `obj`, `two-column` and `comparison` are `twoObj`, `image-beside` is `picTx`, `statement` is `titleOnly`, every other template `cust`), so Change Layout and New Slide list them. The layout has one placeholder per area of the empty layout core composes at the deck size (`composeLayoutAreas`, bled regions to the slide edge): `title` (`ctrTitle` and a `subTitle` below it on `cover` and `section`), `subTitle` for a `subtitle` area, and per body region (named by the region, `idx` 13 plus its position in `regions`) `pic` when it takes only pictures or is a `media` region of pictures and video, `chart`, `tbl` or `media` when it takes only charts, tables or video, `body` when only text and lists, otherwise a content placeholder; RR-11's date, footer and slide-number placeholders and `p:hf` stay, and an `OPF_LAYOUT_V1` tag records the layout's id, group, source, reference, a record hash and the region of every placeholder index. Slides relate to their template's layout (a slide whose regions collapse uses the full layout), and the lone picture, chart or table of a `none` region is bound to its placeholder (`p:ph`, at core's geometry), so Reset, Change Picture and Change Layout move it; blocks of flowing regions, text and captioned or carded objects stay free shapes. RR-72 furniture variants copy the slide's own template layout (`Pillars (no furniture)`). A list core breaks into columns (`listColumns`) is drawn in its columns at core's break points (one shape per line, as every list; not a native `numCol` body, whose breaks would differ), where it used to draw the first column only. `OPF_SLIDE_V1` records a block's `region` pin and the region each root block was drawn in, so pins round-trip. `fromPptx` maps a tagged slide layout back: a slide with no stored layout (added in PowerPoint, or exported without provenance) takes it, a slide whose layout relationship names another OPF layout (Change Layout) takes that one (`layout-changed`), text typed into a region placeholder (which has no `xfrm` of its own) sits at the layout's box and gets a `region` pin where plain binding would move it, and a damaged tag is `invalid-layout-provenance`. Slides on 0.18 records and automatic slides export exactly as before.
